---
name: Night Sound Machine Preferences sections
description: Durable organization of the Preferences navigation and its three settings areas.
---

Preferences is organized as three sidebar-only sub-sections: Night Display, ScreenSaver, and App Settings. Night Display owns session clock options, idle dimming, and session-end alarms. ScreenSaver owns the idle clock display. App Settings owns local library information, privacy/about details, version, reset defaults, and general app defaults such as session volume.

**Why:** Preferences should open to useful content instead of appearing blank, while keeping the sidebar uncluttered everywhere else and leaving room for future settings pages.

**How to apply:** Keep the sub-navigation conditional on the main Preferences page being active, default the selected section to Night Display when Preferences is opened, and add future preference areas as sibling sections rather than expanding one long page.
